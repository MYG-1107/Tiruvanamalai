Pilgrim essentials

Temple visit

Start early to avoid crowds, dress modestly, and keep enough time for darshan and the inner prakaram.

Girivalam guidance

The 14 km parikrama path circles Arunachala hill and is lined with shrines, water points, and rest stops.

Travel planning

Tiruvannamalai is well connected by road from Chennai, Bengaluru, and nearby Tamil Nadu cities.
