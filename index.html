THE SACRED WALK

One hill. One circle. A journey of devotion.

Girivalam — also called Giri Pradakshina — is the traditional walk around Arunachala. The road is about 14 kilometres long. Pilgrims traditionally walk clockwise, keeping the hill on their right.

PLAN WITH CARE

A few things to keep in mind

01

Start at your pace

Give yourself enough time for temple visits, prayers and rest. Festival days can be especially busy.

02

Carry water

Keep drinking water, comfortable clothing and a charged phone with you during the walk.

03

Follow local guidance

Road conditions, diversions and access may change. Follow signs, police directions and temple notices.
