BUSY PILGRIMAGE DAYS

Book with extra care.

Demand rises around Pournami, Karthigai Deepam and other festival periods.

  • Confirm the exact address and check-in time.
  • Ask whether parking, lift access and drinking water are available.
  • Check cancellation terms and total price before paying.
  • Keep your booking confirmation and a contact number.
  • Expect traffic restrictions and allow extra time on festival days.
