Stay options in Tiruvannamalai

Pilgrims can choose from temple guest houses, ashrams, budget lodges, and mid-range hotels. Book in advance for full moon and festival dates.

Accommodation categories

Temple guest houses

Arunachaleswarar Temple Devasthanam guest rooms are close to the main gopurams and ideal for devotees.

Ashrams & spiritual stays

Ramana Maharshi Ashram and other spiritual centers offer simple, serene lodging for meditators.

Budget lodges

Budget rooms are available near the bus stand and girivalam road for short pilgrim stays.

Mid-range hotels

Families often prefer mid-range hotels with dining and parking facilities on the main approach roads.

Homestays

Local homestays provide a calm atmosphere and can include home cooked meals on request.

Overnight rest during Girivalam

Rest halls and choultries along the route open during peak pilgrimage days.

Booking tips

  • Reserve early for Pournami and Karthigai Deepam.
  • Carry a government ID for guest house check-in.
  • Confirm check-in hours if you arrive late at night.
  • Ask about meal options if you need vegetarian-only kitchens.
