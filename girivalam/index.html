YOU ARE NEAR THE MAIN TEMPLE

Important places to see inside

After your main darshan, look for these important shrines and features. Ask temple staff for directions and follow current access notices.

  1. Annamalaiyar — the main Shiva sanctum
  2. Unnamulai Amman — the Goddess shrine
  3. Pathala Lingam — the underground shrine
  4. Kambathu Ilaiyanar — the Murugan shrine associated with the pillar legend
  5. Periya Nandi — the great Nandi facing the sanctum
  6. Sivaganga Theertham and Vinayagar — the sacred tank and nearby shrine
  7. Sambandha Vinayagar — a Vinayagar shrine in the outer courtyard
  8. Gopura Ilayanar — a Murugan shrine near a temple tower
  9. Kalyana Sundareswarar — Shiva in the wedding form
  10. Thousand-Pillared Hall — the historic mandapa
  11. Historic gopurams — Raja, Vallala Maharaja, Kili and Ammani Amman towers
Indoor GPS cannot pinpoint individual shrines. This is a visit checklist, not indoor turn-by-turn navigation.
