Girivalam (Giri Pradakshina) Route

The Girivalam path is a sacred 14 km circumambulation of Arunachala hill. Walk clockwise and pause at the shrines and holy spots along the way.

Key temples and places to visit

Indra Lingam

The eastern shrine marking the starting direction.

Agni Lingam

The south-eastern shrine associated with the element of fire.

Yama Lingam

The southern shrine reminding pilgrims of dharma and time.

Niruthi Lingam

The south-western shrine, a traditional rest stop for devotees.

Varuna Lingam

The western shrine linked to water and purification.

Vayu Lingam

The north-western shrine representing the element of wind.

Kubera Lingam

The northern shrine associated with prosperity and abundance.

Esanya (Ishanya) Lingam

The north-eastern shrine that completes the circuit.

Virupaksha Cave

A revered meditation cave connected to Sri Ramana Maharshi.

Ramana Maharshi Ashram

Quiet satsang halls, meditation spaces, and a peaceful garden.

Sri Seshadri Swamigal Ashram

A traditional stop for pilgrims seeking blessings and rest.

Skandashram

Hilltop ashram with views of Arunachala and the temple town.

Tips for Girivalam

  • Carry water and light snacks, especially during daytime walks.
  • Wear comfortable footwear or walk barefoot as per your vow.
  • Plan for 3-5 hours depending on pace and stop duration.
  • Full moon days (Pournami) are especially significant.
