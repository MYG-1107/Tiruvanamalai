GIRI PRADAKSHINA · ARUNACHALA

Walk around the sacred hill.

Girivalam is a devotional walk around Arunachala, traditionally completed clockwise with the hill on your right. The public road around the hill is approximately 14 km.

BEFORE YOU SET OUT

Walk mindfully and safely.

Many pilgrims complete Girivalam in a few hours, but time depends on pace, crowd conditions and the number of stops.

  • Carry drinking water and take breaks when needed.
  • Prefer cooler hours and be prepared for night walking.
  • Keep the hill on your right and follow local traffic instructions.
  • Wear suitable footwear if the road surface or heat requires it.
  • On Pournami and festival days, expect large crowds and diversions.
  • Keep the path clean and respect the shrines and local residents.
